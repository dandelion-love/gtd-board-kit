# Agile foundations

* [Acceptance criteria, Definition of Done, Definition of Ready](acceptance-criteria-dod-dor.md) - Three different checks with different owners and timing; only the Definition of Done is in Scrum, and a Definition of Ready used as a hard gate is widely criticised
* [INVEST for stories, SMART for tasks](invest-and-smart.md) - Bill Wake's 2003 article gave INVEST for stories and SMART for tasks; SMART itself comes from George Doran's 1981 management article with different letter meanings
* [Where Epic, Story, Theme and Feature come from](origins-of-epic-story-feature.md) - Story and epic come from Extreme Programming teams, the story template from Connextra, the 3 Cs from Ron Jeffries, and Feature from Feature-Driven Development long before SAFe made it a formal level
* [What the Scrum Guide 2020 defines, and what it leaves out](scrum-guide-2020-scope.md) - The 2020 Scrum Guide defines Product Backlog items, the Product Goal, the Sprint Goal and the Definition of Done, but not epics, stories, tasks, features, story points, velocity or a Definition of Ready
* [Sizing work when the builder is an AI agent](sizing-work-for-ai-agents.md) - For agent-built work the useful size limits are reliability at task length, one reviewable pull request, and a checkable done condition; raw context-window size has stopped being the binding limit
* [Story points, their critiques, and flow metrics](story-points-and-estimation-critiques.md) - Story points are a relative effort estimate; their likely inventor regrets their misuse,
* [Story splitting patterns (Humanizing Work, SPIDR) and vertical slicing](story-splitting-patterns.md) - Split stories into thin vertical slices using Humanizing Work's nine patterns or Cohn's five SPIDR techniques, and keep layer splits for tasks and pull requests
* [User stories, the 3 Cs, and job stories](user-stories-and-job-stories.md) - The Connextra template, Jeffries' Card-Conversation-Confirmation, and the job story as an alternative that names the situation instead of the role
* [User story mapping (Patton)](user-story-mapping.md) - A two-dimensional backlog where user activities form a left-to-right backbone, stories hang below them, and horizontal slices mark releases, the first slice being a walking skeleton
* [Walking skeleton and tracer bullet](walking-skeleton-and-tracer-bullet.md) - Build the thinnest end-to-end slice first, linking the main parts of the system, then thicken it; the first story of a new epic should usually be this slice
* [Work dependencies, the dependency graph and the critical path](work-dependencies.md) - Dependency types from scheduling practice, why the graph must be acyclic, what the critical path tells you, and how to record dependencies so ordering can be computed
* [Work item hierarchies compared](work-item-hierarchies-compared.md) - Textbook, SAFe, Jira, Azure DevOps, Linear, Shape Up and Kanban side by side, and when a Feature level between Epic and Story earns its place
