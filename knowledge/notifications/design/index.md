# Notification design (prior art and evidence)

* [AI coding agent notifications](ai-coding-agent-notifications.md) - As of late 2026, hosted coding agents tell the human through the place the task came from (a PR review request, a Slack thread reply, a status emoji), and local agents expose hooks for "needs input" and "done"; none offers urgency tiers or digests
* [Alert fatigue and receptivity](alert-fatigue-and-receptivity.md) - Clinicians accept fewer alerts as the number and share of repeats grow, and phone users find even useful notifications disruptive depending on sender, task and presentation, so volume and repetition must be kept low
* [When dsh-superagent lets an agent speak to a person](dsh-superagent-when-to-speak.md) - DeepSeek's prototype treats every message and notification as a cost; an agent speaks only to acknowledge once, deliver a result, ask one question or report that it cannot finish, in the conversation where it was asked, and everything else waits on a pull panel
* [GitHub notification model lessons](github-notification-model-lessons.md) - Design lessons only from GitHub's subscription model, where every notification carries a reason and the inbox offers done, save, unsubscribe and custom filters; the mechanics are covered on a separate page
* [Interruption cost and breakpoints](interruption-cost-and-breakpoints.md) - Field and lab studies show interruptions raise stress and frustration even when people keep pace, and that holding a notification until the person reaches a task breakpoint cuts frustration at a cost of about a minute and a half of delay
* [Linear inbox and triage](linear-inbox-and-triage.md) - Linear routes subscribed-issue events into an Inbox with a Priority tab and snooze, sends email only if the in-app item is still unread, and keeps a separate Triage queue with accept, decline, duplicate and snooze actions
* [Notification design principles](notification-design-principles.md) - Eight rules, drawn from paging tools, notification platforms, chat and tracker inboxes, coding agents and interruption research, for notifying one busy owner only when they must act
* [Notification infrastructure workflows](notification-infrastructure-workflows.md) - Novu, Knock and Courier model a notification as a workflow of steps (digest or batch, throttle, preference check, channel routing with fallback), which gives the kit a ready vocabulary for its own send logic
* [PagerDuty urgency and escalation](pagerduty-urgency-and-escalation.md) - PagerDuty splits incidents into high and low urgency, escalates only unacknowledged high-urgency work, folds repeat alerts into one incident, and can store events without paging anyone
* [Slack notification controls](slack-notification-controls.md) - Slack lets each person choose everything or only mentions, set keyword and per-channel exceptions, schedule quiet hours, delay mobile pushes while they are active, and let a sender break through Do Not Disturb once a day
